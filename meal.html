Key reminders

  • Breakfast is the same every day — eggs and sausage. Keep it simple and consistent.
  • Tuna with 1–2 tbsp mayo is totally fine. Use it as a binder, not a scoop.
  • Skip the bun on burgers and the shell on tacos — saves ~150 cal with no loss of fullness.
  • Fish days (Wed, Sat): salmon is the best choice — omega-3s help with recovery from running.
  • Aim for 130–140g protein daily to preserve muscle while losing fat.
  • Greek yogurt (plain, full fat) is one of the best snacks — high protein, filling, no sugar.
  • Drink at least 10 cups of water daily — hunger is often just dehydration.
  • Prep extra at Sunday dinner for Monday — makes the week much easier.
