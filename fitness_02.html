General reminders

  • 5 min dynamic warmup before every run: leg swings, high knees, hip circles
  • Stretch calves and hamstrings after every run to prevent shin splints
  • Run 2 min / walk 1 min in weeks 1–2, then build to continuous running
  • Off days are where tendons and joints adapt — don't skip them
  • Coffee with half & half before your run is totally fine
  • Weigh yourself weekly, same time each morning
  • Expect 1.5–2.5 lb loss per week with good adherence

Key reminders

  • Breakfast every morning — eggs and sausage. Don't skip it, it fuels your workout and controls hunger.
  • Snack is your bridge — make it count with at least 20–25g protein or you'll overeat at dinner.
  • Tuna with 1–2 tbsp mayo is a perfect snack — quick, high protein, no cooking.
  • Dinner with the kids: skip the bun, skip the shell, skip heavy sauces. Eat the same food, just cleaner.
  • Salmon is the best fish — omega-3s help recovery from running and lifting.
  • Never drop below 1,400 cal/day — muscle loss kicks in below that.
  • Drink at least 10 cups of water daily — hunger is often just dehydration.
  • Greek yogurt and hard-boiled eggs are your best snack friends — prep them Sunday.
