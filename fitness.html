General reminders

  • 5 min dynamic warmup before every run: leg swings, high knees, hip circles
  • Stretch calves and hamstrings after every run to prevent shin splints
  • Run 2 min / walk 1 min in weeks 1–2, then build to continuous running
  • Off days aren't laziness — tendons and joints adapt on rest days
  • Coffee with half & half before your run is totally fine
  • Weigh yourself weekly, same time each morning
  • Expect 1.5–2.5 lb loss per week with good adherence

Key reminders

  • Breakfast is the same every day — eggs and sausage. Consistent and simple.
  • Tuna with 1–2 tbsp mayo is totally fine — use it as a binder, not a scoop.
  • Skip the bun on burgers and shell on tacos — saves ~150 cal with no loss of fullness.
  • Salmon is the best fish choice — omega-3s help recovery from running and lifting.
  • Greek yogurt (plain, full fat) is one of the best snacks — high protein and filling.
  • Drink at least 10 cups of water daily — hunger is often just dehydration.
  • Never drop below 1,600 cal/day — muscle loss kicks in below that.
  • Prep extra at Sunday dinner for Monday — makes the week much easier.
